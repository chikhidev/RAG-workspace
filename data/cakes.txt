========================================================================
                 THE ART AND SCIENCE OF CAKES
========================================================================

A cake is a form of sweet food made from flour, sugar, and other 
ingredients, that is usually baked. In their oldest forms, cakes were 
modifications of bread, but cakes now cover a wide range of 
preparations.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. CORE INGREDIENTS
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Most standard cakes rely on a specific balance of these four elements:

* Flour: Provides the structure (gluten).
* Eggs: Act as a binder and provide volume.
* Fat (Butter/Oil): Adds moisture and carries flavor.
* Sugar: Sweetens and softens the texture by slowing gluten growth.
* Leavening Agents: Baking soda or powder create the rise.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. THE TWO MAIN CATEGORIES
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Most cakes fall into one of two baking methods:

A. Shortened (Butter) Cakes
   These contain fat (butter, margarine, or shortening). They are 
   moist, dense, and flavorful. 
   Examples: Pound cake, Red Velvet, Carrot cake.

B. Foam (Sponge) Cakes
   These contain little to no fat and a high proportion of eggs. The 
   rise comes primarily from air whipped into the eggs.
   Examples: Angel Food cake, Chiffon, Genovese sponge.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. POPULAR FROSTINGS AND FINISHES
------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Buttercream: A mix of butter and powdered sugar; the most common 
  decorating medium.
* Ganache: A rich glaze made from chocolate and heavy cream.
* Fondant: A pliable "sugar dough" used for smooth, sculpted designs.
* Royal Icing: A hard white icing used for intricate detailing.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. FUN CAKE TRIVIA
------------------------------------------------------------------------
* The word "cake" is of Viking origin, from the Old Norse word "kaka."
* In ancient times, cakes were often offered to the gods and spirits 
  as a sign of reverence.
* The world's tallest cake was over 100 feet high, built for a 
  festival in Indonesia.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. BAKING TIPS FOR SUCCESS
------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Room Temperature: Ensure eggs and butter are at room temperature 
  unless the recipe says otherwise to ensure proper emulsification.
* Don't Overmix: Once the flour is added, mix only until combined to 
  avoid a tough, rubbery texture.
* Oven Calibration: Always preheat your oven; an oven thermometer is 
  the best tool for accuracy.
