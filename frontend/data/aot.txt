========================================================================
             ATTACK ON TITAN: THE COMPLETE STORY SUMMARY
========================================================================

"Attack on Titan" (Shingeki no Kyojin) is a generational epic that 
evolves from a simple survival story into a complex political drama 
revolving around war, freedom, and the cycle of hatred.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. THE PREMISE: LIFE INSIDE THE WALLS
------------------------------------------------------------------------
For 100 years, the last of humanity has lived inside three massive 
walls—Maria, Rose, and Sina—to hide from man-eating giants called 
Titans. 

In the year 845, the "Colossal Titan" and "Armored Titan" breach the 
outermost wall. During the chaos, young Eren Yeager witnesses his 
mother being eaten. He vows to join the Survey Corps and "kill every 
last one of them."

------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. THE TITAN SHIFTERS AND THE BASEMENT
------------------------------------------------------------------------
As the war progresses, several major revelations occur:
* Eren discovers he can transform into a Titan (the Attack Titan).
* Other humans are "Shifters" from within the military, including 
  Annie (Female Titan), Reiner (Armored), and Bertholdt (Colossal).
* The Truth: Upon reaching his father’s basement in Shiganshina, Eren 
  learns that humanity is NOT extinct. The people inside the walls are 
  Eldians (Subjects of Ymir), a race that can turn into Titans.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. THE GLOBAL CONFLICT (MARLEY VS. ELDIA)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
The world outside is 19th-century level technology. The nation of 
Marley has used Eldians as second-class citizens and turned them into 
Titans to keep the "Island of Paradis" (where the walls are) 
imprisoned.

Marley wants the "Founding Titan" (held by Eren) to maintain military 
dominance, while the Eldians on Paradis simply want to survive.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. THE ORIGIN: YMIR THE FOUNDER
------------------------------------------------------------------------
2,000 years ago, a slave girl named Ymir gained the power of the 
Titans after contacting a mysterious "source of all living matter." 
She was used by King Fritz to build an empire. Upon her death, her 
soul was trapped in a metaphysical dimension called "The Paths," 
where she continued to build Titans out of sand for eternity.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. THE ENDGAME: THE RUMBLING
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Eren, seeing no other way to ensure his friends' survival against a 
world that hates them, triggers "The Rumbling." He unhardens the 
millions of Colossal Titans inside the walls and sends them to 
trample the entire world.

The finale follows his former friends (Mikasa, Armin, Levi) as they 
team up with their former enemies to stop Eren from committing 
global genocide.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. THE CONCLUSION
------------------------------------------------------------------------
* The Sacrifice: Mikasa kills Eren, which provides the closure the 
  Founder Ymir needed to let go of her attachment to the world.
* The Result: The power of the Titans vanishes from the world, and all 
  Titans turn back into humans.
* The Message: While the "Titan threat" ends, the credits show that 
  human nature remains the same. Thousands of years later, war returns, 
  suggesting the cycle of conflict is a permanent part of humanity.
