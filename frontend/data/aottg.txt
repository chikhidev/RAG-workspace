========================================================================
            ATTACK ON TITAN TRIBUTE GAME 2 (AoTTG2) OVERVIEW
========================================================================

AoTTG2 is the community-driven, fan-made sequel to the original 
"Attack on Titan Tribute Game" by Feng Lee. It aims to modernize the 
classic high-octane physics-based gameplay of the first game while 
adding significant technical improvements.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. CORE GAMEPLAY FEATURES
------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Advanced ODM Gear: Realistic, physics-based movement allows players 
  to swing, reel, and boost through the air with dual grapples.
* Titan Combat: Slay various Titan types—including Normals, Abnormals, 
  and Crawlers—by striking the nape of their neck.
* Weapon Variety: Includes classic blades as well as modern options 
  like Thunder Spears for heavy damage.
* Character Customization: A robust system to create your own soldier 
  with custom faces, hair, and military gear.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. TECHNICAL ENHANCEMENTS
------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Engine Upgrade: Built on a modern engine (Unity), offering better 
  performance, lighting, and textures compared to the original browser 
  version.
* Cross-Platform: Available as a standalone download for Windows, 
  macOS, and Linux.
* Launcher: Features an auto-updating launcher to ensure players 
  always have the latest balance patches and content.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. MULTIPLAYER AND MODES
------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Global Servers: Dedicated regions for North America, Europe, South 
  America, China, and Asia.
* PvP and Co-op: Battle against other players in skill-based modes or 
  team up with friends to clear maps of Titan infestations.
* Map Editor: Allows the community to build and share custom 
  battlefields, ranging from the Trost District to the Forest of 
  Giant Trees.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. HOW TO GET STARTED
------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Official Site: https://aottg2.com/
* Itch.io: The game is hosted on Itch.io for secure downloads.
* Community: The project is supported by a large Discord community 
  where players can report bugs and share strategies.
* Development: The game is in "ongoing development" (Alpha/Beta state), 
  meaning it receives regular updates and new features.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. QUICK CONTROLS (DEFAULT)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Q / E: Left and Right Hook.
* Space: Both Hooks.
* W/A/S/D: Air movement and swing control.
* Left Click: Attack / Slash.
* Left Shift: Gas Boost.
* R: Reload Blades.
